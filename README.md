## 🎯 Overview

The Body Jewelry Size Visualizer is an interactive tool designed for clients and piercers to preview how different lengths and diameters of jewelry will appear on different areas of the body. This helps in selecting the most appropriate size for both aesthetics and comfort.

**Key Use Cases:**

- Diameter visualization for hoops (lips, nose, ears)
- Length visualization for bars (tongue, industrial, etc.)
- Gauge comparison for various piercings

**Target Audience:**

- Piercing professionals
- Clients planning new piercings or jewelry upgrades

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Interactive Sizing** - Adjust diameter and length to see real-time visual changes.
- ✅ **Multiple Anatomy Options** - Choose from common piercing locations.
- ✅ **Standard units** - Works with millimeters and inches.
- ✅ **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- ✅ **Free & Open Source** - MIT licensed.
