## What is Anatomical Jewelry Visualizer?

The Anatomical Jewelry Visualizer is a free web tool that renders body jewelry at true 1:1 scale on your screen. It uses screen calibration to ensure accurate size representation, displaying both imperial (gauge, inches) and metric (millimeters) measurements. The tool includes a comprehensive jewelry database with categories including rings, labrets, barbells, circular barbells, ball closure rings, clickers, and plugs/tunnels. Users can filter by gauge size (20g through 00g), material (titanium, surgical steel, gold, niobium, glass, stone, acrylic), and body location. The visualizer features six main tabs: Interactive Visualizer, Virtual Try-On, Size Comparison, Body Guide, Style Selector, and Stretching Guide. The virtual try-on feature allows users to upload a photo and overlay jewelry with adjustable scale, rotation, and opacity controls. The size comparison tool enables side-by-side comparison of up to 4 jewelry items with a detailed specification table and European paper size standards reference.

## How to Use

**Step 1: Calibrate Your Screen**
Click "Calibrate Now" on the calibration banner that appears at the top of the visualizer. Follow on-screen instructions to ensure 1:1 scale accuracy.

**Step 2: Filter Jewelry by Category**
Use the Category dropdown to select from: Rings, Labrets & Studs, Barbells, Circular Barbells & Horseshoes, Ball Closure Rings (BCR), Clickers & Seamless, or Plugs & Tunnels.

**Step 3: Select Gauge Size**
Choose from the Gauge dropdown: 20g (0.8mm) through 00g (10.0mm). The tool shows both gauge number and millimeter equivalent.

**Step 4: Choose Material**
Filter by material: Implant-Grade Titanium, Surgical Steel, 14k/18k Gold, Niobium, Glass, Stone, or Acrylic.

**Step 5: Filter by Body Location**
Select from: Ear, Nose, Lip, Tongue, Eyebrow, Navel, Nipple, Surface, or Genital (Male/Female).

**Step 6: View 1:1 Scale Preview**
Click any jewelry item in the gallery to open the detail view. The canvas shows the jewelry at actual size with a ruler reference.

**Step 7: Compare Multiple Sizes**
Switch to the Size Compare tab. Click empty slots to add jewelry items. Compare up to 4 items side-by-side with a detailed specification table.

**Step 8: Virtual Try-On**
Switch to the Virtual Try-On tab. Upload a photo of the piercing area. Use scale, rotation, and opacity controls to position jewelry accurately.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How accurate is the 1:1 scale display?**
A: The tool requires screen calibration for accurate 1:1 scale. A calibration banner appears on first use. After calibration, jewelry renders at actual size based on your screen's DPI.

**Q: What gauge sizes are available?**
A: The tool supports gauges from 20g (0.8mm) through 00g (10.0mm), including 18g, 16g, 14g, 12g, 10g, 8g, 6g, 4g, 2g, and 0g.

**Q: Can I compare multiple jewelry sizes at once?**
A: Yes. The Size Comparison tab lets you add up to 4 jewelry items and view them side-by-side with a detailed specification table.

**Q: How does the virtual try-on work?**
A: Upload a clear photo of the piercing area. The tool overlays jewelry on your photo with adjustable scale (50-150%), rotation (0-360°), and opacity (10-100%) controls.

**Q: What body locations are covered in the anatomy guide?**
A: The guide covers 10 locations: Ear, Nose, Lip, Tongue, Eyebrow, Navel, Nipple, Surface, Male Genital, and Female Genital. Each includes specific gauge, size, and healing recommendations.

**Q: What materials can I filter by?**
A: Filter by Implant-Grade Titanium, Surgical Steel, 14k/18k Gold, Niobium, Glass, Stone, or Acrylic.

**Q: Can I embed this tool on my website?**
A: Yes. The Embed Code tab provides iframe code with customizable height options (600px, 800px, 1000px). The tool works on HTTP and HTTPS with no external API calls.

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+, iOS Safari 14+, and Android Chrome 90+.

**Q: Does the tool collect any data?**
A: No. The tool makes no external API calls, uses no cookies, and collects no data. Camera access requires user permission for virtual try-on.

**Q: What is the difference between initial and healed jewelry sizes?**
A: Initial jewelry is longer to accommodate swelling. For example, tongue piercings start at 5/8"-3/4" (16-19mm) and downsize to 1/2"-5/8" (12-16mm) after healing.
